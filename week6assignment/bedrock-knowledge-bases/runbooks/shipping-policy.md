# Northwind Shipping Policy

## Service levels

| Service | Delivery target | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5-7 business days | Free over $75, otherwise $6.95 |
| Expedited | 2-3 business days | $18.50 |
| Overnight | Next business day if ordered before 14:00 UTC | $39.00 |

## Cut-off times

Orders placed after **14:00 UTC** are processed the following business day.
Overnight shipping ordered after the cut-off will arrive in two business days,
not one.

## Lost or delayed shipments

A shipment is considered lost after **10 business days** past its delivery
target. At that point the customer may choose a replacement at no cost or a
full refund including original shipping.

## International

We ship to 14 countries. Customs duties are the customer's responsibility and
are not included in the quoted price. International returns must be initiated
within 60 days rather than 45.
