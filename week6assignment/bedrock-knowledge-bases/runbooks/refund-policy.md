# Northwind Returns and Refund Policy

Effective 2026-01-15. Supersedes all prior versions.

## Standard return window

Customers may return most items within **45 days** of the delivery date. The
window is measured from delivery, not from the order date.

## Restocking fee

Returns received after day 30 but within the 45-day window are subject to a
**12% restocking fee**, deducted from the refund. Returns received on or
before day 30 carry no restocking fee.

## Non-returnable items

- Digital licence keys, once revealed
- Custom-configured hardware
- Items marked "Final sale" at the time of purchase

## Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within **7 business
days** of the returned item arriving at our warehouse. Customers who paid by
bank transfer should allow an additional 3 business days.

## Exceptions

A support agent may waive the restocking fee if the return is caused by a
defect, a shipping error, or a documented service outage affecting the order.
Waivers above $500 require a supervisor's approval.
