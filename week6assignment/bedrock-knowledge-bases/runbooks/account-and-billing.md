# Account and Billing Reference

## Plan tiers

| Plan | Seats included | SSO | Monthly price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 10 | No | $240 |
| Professional | 50 | Yes (SAML) | $900 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Yes (SAML + SCIM) | Custom |

**SSO is not available on the Standard plan.** Customers asking about SSO on
Standard must upgrade to Professional or above.

## Seat overages

Additional seats beyond the plan allowance are billed at $22 per seat per
month, prorated to the day.

## Unused seats

Unused seats **do not roll over** between billing periods. This is the single
most common billing question we receive.

## Billing cycle

Invoices are generated on the first day of each month and payment is due
within 30 days. Accounts more than 45 days past due are suspended, not
cancelled; data is retained for 90 days after suspension.
